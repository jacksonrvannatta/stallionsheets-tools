How to price a house cleaning job

Many cleaners price by the job, but the number behind it is time. This calculator estimates the labor hours a home will take, multiplies them by your hourly rate and adds a supplies fee. The result is a starting point for your quote.

Square feet and rooms

Square footage gives a rough measure of how much floor and surface there is to clean. Bathrooms and kitchens usually take longer per square foot, so the calculator adds extra minutes for each one. Both the pace and the extra minutes are yours to set, since every cleaner works differently.

Find your own pace

Time your next several jobs and note the home size and the hours spent. Dividing square feet by hours gives your real pace. Use that number instead of the starting value and your quotes will match your work.

Your hourly rate

Your rate should cover more than wages: fuel, supplies, insurance, taxes and unpaid time spent quoting and driving. If you are not sure what to enter, try the hourly rate calculator first.

Before you send a quote

Deep cleans, pets, heavy buildup, stairs and move-out jobs often take longer than a standard visit. Adjust your pace or add time for those. Compare the result with what other cleaners in your area charge and decide on your final price.

Frequently asked questions

How do I price a house cleaning by square foot?

Divide the square feet by how many square feet you clean per hour, add time for bathrooms and the kitchen, then multiply by your hourly rate. This calculator does those steps for you.

What pace should I enter?

Use your own. Time a few real jobs and divide the square feet by the hours it took. The starting value is only a placeholder.

Does the price include travel?

No. Add travel time or a trip fee yourself if you charge for it.

Is this the price I must charge?

No. It is a starting estimate. Check it against your costs and local prices.
