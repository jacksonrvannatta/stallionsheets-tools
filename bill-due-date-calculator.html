How to total your monthly bills

Bills do not all arrive monthly. Some are weekly, some quarterly and some yearly. To compare them, each one is converted to a monthly amount: a yearly bill is divided by 12, a quarterly bill by 3, and an every-two-weeks bill is multiplied by 26 and divided by 12.

Why per paycheck matters

If you are paid every two weeks, two paychecks a month is only an approximation, because 26 paychecks make 2.17 a month on average. This calculator uses the average, so the amount to set aside per paycheck covers your bills over the whole year. In months with three paychecks you have extra to work with.

Using due days

Enter the day of the month each bill is due and the results list sorts them in order, so you can see which bills land before your next payday. Due days are most useful for monthly bills.

Covering irregular bills

Yearly and quarterly bills such as insurance and subscriptions are easy to forget. Including them here shows the monthly amount you should be saving so they do not surprise you.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a yearly bill to monthly?

Divide it by 12. Quarterly bills are divided by 3, weekly bills are multiplied by 52 and divided by 12, and every-two-weeks bills by 26 and 12.

Why is per paycheck not just half the monthly total?

Only twice-a-month pay gives exactly two paychecks. Every two weeks gives 26 a year, or about 2.17 a month on average.

Does this send reminders?

No. It only does the math. Nothing you enter is saved or sent.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a planning estimate based on the numbers you enter.
